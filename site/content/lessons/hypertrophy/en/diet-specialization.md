# Diet phases and specialization

Recovery conditions determine how much useful work can be supported.

## The adaptive window changes {#diet-specialization-diet}

The book’s model proposes a wider MEV–MRV window during a calorie surplus and a narrower one during a deficit. Recovery and growth are more constrained during fat loss, and maintaining muscle becomes the relevant success criterion, especially for trained lifters. Do not interpret a smaller window as permission to compensate with unlimited extra effort.

At maintenance, a low-volume resensitization phase may be appropriate when that is the planned goal. During fat loss, the authors often favor high-SFR choices and cautious progression as fatigue increases. Exact landmark shifts cannot be calculated from calorie balance alone. Record the person’s actual performance and tissue response rather than assigning automatic set changes.

> Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training, ch. 7, EPUB Text/part0022.xhtml, Individualization of Training for Different Diet Phases

## Prioritize some muscles and maintain others {#diet-specialization-specialize}

Specialization trains priority muscles for growth while holding others near maintenance. It can solve a systemic bottleneck when every muscle cannot simultaneously receive a productive growth dose. Because MV is often substantially below MEV in trained lifters, lowering non-priority work can free meaningful resources while preserving prior gains.

Do not leave non-priority muscles at a vague in-between dose that adds fatigue without robust growth, or neglect them below maintenance. Rotate priorities deliberately and allow the priority muscles low-stress periods too. The source does not generally recommend growth specialization during a deficit, where growth opportunities are already limited. Beginners usually do not need this strategy.

> Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training, ch. 7, EPUB Text/part0022.xhtml, Specialization Phases; Specialization Phases Across Training Age

## Recap

- A fat-loss phase changes both the goal and the recovery context.
- Specialization reallocates resources while preserving other muscles.
