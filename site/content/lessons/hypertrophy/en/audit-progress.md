# Audit a stalled program

Troubleshoot the whole framework before changing one favorite variable.

## A principles-first audit {#audit-progress-audit}

Check specificity first: is the target muscle actually trained in a growth-promoting way? Then inspect dose, effort and progression, followed by fatigue, frequency, variation and phase structure. A complex schedule cannot rescue insufficient stimulus, while harder training cannot solve chronic under-recovery.

The source’s troubleshooting list also includes nutrition, sleep, consistent execution, resensitization where appropriate, patience and realistic expectations. Compare performance under similar conditions across mesocycles. Muscular change is often too slow and measurement error too large to interpret day-to-day fluctuations as tissue gain or loss.

> Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training, ch. 8, EPUB Text/part0025.xhtml, Troubleshooting Lack of Progress; Measuring Progress

## Evidence that can and cannot establish progress {#audit-progress-measure}

Rep strength over several well-executed mesocycles is useful indirect evidence, but skill and altered execution confound it. Pump, RSM and SFR support decisions about the training process rather than proving final growth. Photographs and circumference measures require consistent conditions; body-composition tools have their own precision limits.

**Original audit example:** a trainee reports a plateau after two weeks but has changed exercises, increased momentum and weighed in at different times. First standardize the comparison and continue an appropriate observation period. Do not automatically add volume to solve an apparent plateau that the records cannot yet establish.

## Recap

- Investigate fundamentals in order of importance.
- Standardize measurements and allow an appropriate timescale.
